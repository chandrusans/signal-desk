The verdicts on each position (SELL / TRIM / HOLD / WATCH) are mechanical outputs of the same momentum-swing framework used on the Stocks page, applied to your specific entry price. They are decision-support, not orders. You are still the one clicking the sell button. Export the JSON regularly if you value your data. See the Handbook Dashboard chapter for how the verdicts are computed.
