What Signal Desk is

Signal Desk is a research and practice tool. It applies a published set of mechanical rules (written out in the Handbook) to market data and shows the result: scores, setups, example entry and exit levels, position sizes worked out from the numbers you type in, and alerts.

What it is not

  • It is not investment advice. Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. The same rules produce the same output for every user; the app does not know your finances, goals or circumstances and does not take them into account. Words such as "buy signal" describe a rule being met, not a judgement that you should trade.
  • We are not your adviser or broker. We are not registered as an investment adviser or broker-dealer, we owe you no fiduciary duty, and no adviser-client relationship is created by using the app.
  • It places no real orders. "Paper" trades are pretend entries in your own browser. If the operator connects a paper-trading account at a broker, the app can send pretend-money orders to that paper account only; it refuses live trading keys. The app cannot move money or trade real securities on your behalf.

The record of these rules

We publish how the rules performed. Run day by day over ten years of the S&P 500, the Swing rules roughly broke even while simply holding the index returned about 14% a year (see the Swing page). Past results, tested or real, do not predict future results. You can lose some or all of the money you put into any investment, including by following these rules exactly.

Data

Market data comes from third parties and can be late, incomplete or wrong. Earnings dates marked as estimates are estimates. The app can be unavailable, and alerts can arrive late or not at all. Check anything that matters against your broker or the original source before acting on it. Third-party data is provided for personal, non-commercial use under those providers' own terms.
