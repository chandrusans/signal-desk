Signal Desk · Privacy

What we keep, and where.

Draft for legal review. This page describes how the app works today, written plainly. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before the app is offered to other people, the highlighted blanks need filling in and a qualified lawyer should review it for the places where it will be used.

Signal Desk is operated by [operator name] ("we"). This page says what information the app handles, where it is kept, and what you can do about it. Questions: [contact email].

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • What you enter (positions, journal, lists, settings) is kept in your own browser. It leaves your device only if you switch on one of two optional features: sync or phone alerts.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell or share information.
  • The app never asks for a broker login and never places a real order. An optional paper-trading connection sends pretend-money orders (ticker, size, stop and target) to Alpaca, under Alpaca's own terms, using keys the operator keeps on the server.

Market data and other services

Prices, news, filings and calendars come from third parties: Yahoo Finance, NASDAQ, the US Securities and Exchange Commission, FairEconomy, the Clerk of the US House of Representatives and Coinbase. Your browser does not contact them. It asks our server, and our server asks them, so they see our server's address and the ticker symbol, not you.

The site is hosted by Vercel and its scheduled jobs run on GitHub Actions. Neither receives the data you enter, apart from the storage described in the table above.

Cookies

The app sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember what you enter, which stays on your device unless you turn on sync or phone alerts.

Your choices

  • See or move your data: Dashboard → Export downloads it as a file.
  • Delete it from a device: clear this site's data in your browser settings, or use the reset and clear buttons in the app.
  • Stop sync: Dashboard → Sync → "Turn off on this device", or "Delete the cloud copy" to remove the stored copy for every device.
  • Stop phone alerts: Alerts → "Turn off push here", or remove the channel details on the Alerts page and save. To have the stored alert data deleted, write to [contact email].

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have legal rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to complain to a regulator. Because the app has no accounts, the practical route is the controls above; for anything else, write to us and we will respond within [response period, e.g. 30 days].

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Synced data is encrypted on your device before it is sent. Nothing protects data on a device that someone else can unlock, and a sync passphrase cannot be recovered if you forget it. No system is perfectly secure; do not store anything here that you could not afford to have exposed.

Children

The app is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18.
